Grey = not available today (rest of the last 5h window, or the daily share cut by the 7d limit or custom cap). Orange = today almost used up but not yet: today low, today short, cap close, current 5h window full. Red = today's allowance reached or over, a cap reached or over, or a limit used up. Neutral grey pill = no budget state (day off, not ready, unknown, period unknown, not included, signed out). OK has no pill: a card without a pill is on track (accessible name still says OK). Day off (O): the today strip keeps the share today would get as a work day, drawn neutral with a dashed outline and never orange or red; an exhausted limit stays red. Work today (window-wide, until midnight, settings unchanged) colours the same strip as on a work day. Rush (R): on the last work day before a provider-replenished reset with no custom cap, today is the whole remainder: green “rush” pill, no 7d or month bar, no grey. Money, credits and capped pools never rush. Used up (a provider limit, not a custom cap, fully used): no today strip, only the red 7d or month bar and when it comes back; this wins over rush. ≈ marks an estimate; (assumed) marks a period the app assumes. Dimmed bars = stale reading. No “5h + 7d” or “7d” tag next to the name: the current 5h cell carries a small “5h” label, and the bar row is labelled 7d. Hover bars, pills, marks and footers for tooltips.
